# Product

## Mission

The Mahjong Room teaches American Mahjong by placing a patient, contextual coach beside a beginner during real play. The coach explains what is happening, what choices are available, and why a choice is useful, then becomes less visible as skill grows.

## MVP promise

A guest can begin without an account, identify tiles, complete a guided Charleston, make real draw and discard decisions against three deterministic bots, inspect three candidate Training Card hands, request progressive hints, and finish with an educational review.

## Non-goals

The MVP does not include multiplayer, payments, matchmaking, chat, native apps, an LLM coach, or proprietary annual card content.

## Success measure

The primary activation event is completing the first guided game. The longer-term north star is the percentage of beginners who complete five games.
